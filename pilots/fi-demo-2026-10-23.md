## Ballot policy and acceptance criteria

- One eligible registration per resolved applicant identity under the demo policy;
  the sandbox does not prove universal unique personhood.
- Every eligible entry has one equal chance; no weighting for eloquence, follower
  count, optional World ID or previous purchases. Any curated allocation would
  require a separately disclosed pool and is outside this demonstration.
- Unresolved linked duplicates, held cases and incomplete checks are excluded
  with reasons. Clearing a weak flag does not bypass other required checks.
- Show eligible/pending/held counts before freezing. Snapshot IDs, opportunity
  count, policy and timestamp must match the actual draw input.
- Use browser cryptographic randomness and select without replacement. Six
  distinct winners and the remaining ranked waitlist must account for the pool.
- Freeze review inputs for the draw; no redraw or extra opportunities on repeat
  clicks. Explicit reset starts a new demo run.
- A local audit trail is illustrative, not a durable or independently verifiable
  lottery record. Never rig a live draw; label any recorded result as recorded.

## Questions for Fi

Ask about actual examples before proposing particular checks:

1. On the last difficult presale, was the greatest pain fake registrations, manual
   shortlisting, leaked codes, checkout or resale? What work or loss resulted?
2. What can reviewers currently see, which tools screen registrations already,
   and what decisions still require manual judgment?
3. Does inspecting related registrations help? What would make a cluster useful
   evidence rather than noise? How should innocent cases be cleared?
4. Who sets eligibility, resolves exceptions and owns allocation? Would an equal
   ballot fit the artist's policy? What fan friction and support are acceptable?
5. Would reviewed cohorts plus an export/handoff help independently, or is seller
   integration essential? Who controls codes, inventory and buyer binding?
6. Who would sponsor and pay for this? What implementation/support burden is viable?
7. Could a permissioned sample or small presale test one hypothesis? What baseline
   and measurable result would justify continuing?
