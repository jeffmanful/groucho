# Mo conversation quality

Before any conversational feature or fix, ask: **Will this positively or negatively affect the conversation experience?** A mechanically valid turn is not sufficient to release. Review complete exchanges for continuity, curiosity, contribution, attribution, corrections and a respectful route to the intended outcome.

## Simplification, 6 November 2026

Expected positive effect: replace overlapping prose rules, question catalogues, regex topic routing, forced questions and mandatory cultural fields with one character brief. Let Mo contribute relevant band context, keep track of the visitor and other people separately, and organically help visitors choose a tour night.

The ticket outcome still matters. Travel origin and exact show night determine whether a preference is complete. Other music, discovery, connection and company are optional conversational information. A visitor may stop with an incomplete preference. Choosing a destination never changes travel origin.

Mo now receives the full transcript within a 40-answer budget, with a considerate wrap-up instruction from 36. Quoted memory notes distinguish visitor, other, correction and thread. Notes can quote earlier visitor statements; assistant assumptions cannot serve as evidence. Display-field updates require current-message evidence and visitor attribution. These checks cannot independently prove semantic truth; review the wording and ownership of facts.

The first simplified Haiku replays passed some mechanical checks but still invented small band anecdotes, repeated an answered outcome and returned a placeholder. They were not sufficient to release. The final contract validates malformed placeholder replies without rewriting legitimate prose. It also handles polite stops and avoids the old consent questionnaire for ordinary music talk.

Sonnet 5.5 was selected for Mo after comparison with Haiku 5.5 on the same anonymised incidents. Sonnet showed better correction handling, attribution and grounded contribution. The tradeoff is higher model cost and slower replies; this is a targeted Mo choice, not a change to other profiles.

Validation included four real-model scenarios: recovery at the original partner/newcomer corrections; a fresh newcomer conversation continuing beyond eleven answers; an unfolding gig story with a direct band-history question; and the original short connection answers. A separate real-model walkthrough verified an organic route from Kitchen Light to Bristol travel origin and London, Sat 17 Apr, including exact date options and simulation completion. All five scenarios passed. Transcripts were manually inspected in addition to assertions. The local library suite passed 764 tests.

These are bounded samples, not proof that every future conversation will be good. Future changes must assess full exchanges again, particularly ambiguous replies, newcomers, people who change topic, corrections, and returning to the ticket choice after a story.


## Date-choice clarity

A gig-date choice or confirmation is a standalone turn. Its registered question and date buttons are the sole request; no conversational or travel question accompanies them. A single-city/single-night choice explicitly asks whether the visitor wants that show. After the selection, Mo acknowledges the date and can return to an unfinished story. The full visitor message and grounded thread notes remain available, so isolating the choice does not discard the conversation. This improves decision clarity without reinstating a general question script or trimming ordinary replies.


The opening answer buttons are retained as an accessible way to start: A gig, A track, Someone brought me along, and I've only just found you. Free text remains available. These starter choices do not select a fixed sequence of follow-up questions.
