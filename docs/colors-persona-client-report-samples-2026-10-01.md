## Calibration observations

1. The source verifier worked: every claim remained tied to an allowed applicant
   evidence statement and no safety allegation was invented.
2. Recommendation calibration did not work consistently: the active artist and
   thoughtful listener were downgraded despite both being client-confirmed
   positive anchors.
3. Several reservations overvalue demonstrated history, breadth, reach, or public
   output. Those are neutral omissions in the calibration policy and should not
   become reasons to withhold a recommendation.
4. The next report-quality slice should supply the reviewer with calibration
   anchors and neutral-missing-information policy, then add an outcome-calibration
   verifier alongside the existing factual-support verifier.
